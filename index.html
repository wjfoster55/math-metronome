How to play with a layer: tap its squares to flip a pulse on / off / accent. Or skip the tapping — use a generator: Grouping (type 4+4+4+3, hits land on each group's first pulse), Every K (a hit every K pulses), Euclid K of N (K hits spread as evenly as math allows over N pulses — the classic polyrhythm machine). Mirror flips a layer backwards; Rotate spins it by one pulse.
